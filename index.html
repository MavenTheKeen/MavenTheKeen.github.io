See it

Torontrack is live and anyone can use it. It has four maps and a findings page, all on open data, tracked continuously since October 2026. It installs as an offline web app and makes no third-party requests.

These are drawings of what each part does, not live data. The real thing is on the live site.

  • Transit

    Live map of TTC, GO, Brampton, MiWay and YRT, with a trip planner and reliability reports. More than 9,000 surface stops have arrival estimates.

  • Food

    About 14,000 places with Toronto Public Health inspection results and licence history.

  • City

    Rinks, pools, beaches, patios and events.

  • Weather

    Radar, wind and air quality.

  • Findings

    Data analyses, such as rain against Bike Share trips and snow against subway delays.

How it works

I built Torontrack for two groups: riders who want to know where their train is, and anyone who wants to know how reliable the service is. A long-running collector polls the TTC's GTFS-Realtime feeds every 10 seconds, around the clock. Other collectors bring in Bike Share, Metrolinx, City of Toronto and Environment Canada data. Everything is stored raw first, then checked, modelled and served.

  1. Feeds

    • TTC GTFS-Realtime
    • Bike Share
    • Metrolinx
    • City of Toronto

  2. Collectors

    Poll each feed on its own schedule. TTC every 10 seconds, Bike Share every 15 minutes.

  3. PostgreSQL, raw first

    Each response is stored before it is parsed. More than a dozen checks reject or flag records, and each one is stored with its reason.

  4. dbt

    29 models with 100+ data tests.

  5. FastAPI and OpenTripPlanner

    A read-only, cached API and a trip planner for Toronto, Brampton, Mississauga and York Region.

  6. The site

    Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step.

Tests
441 automated tests on recorded feed responses, including PostgreSQL integration tests and a full dbt build, in GitHub Actions on every push. See the test runs
Pipeline page
For each feed: last success, runs completed against runs expected, data age, and the records rejected or flagged in the last hour, with reasons. A job can run and still deliver nothing, so health is judged by how fresh the data is.
Between refreshes
Trains move toward their next predicted station. Buses move at the route's usual pace for the hour. Checked against 630,000 position reports, this roughly halves the error.
Trip planner
OpenTripPlanner 2.10, built from five schedules. For frequent TTC service it suggests when to leave to arrive on time about 9 times in 10. How the planner works
Open data
Daily datasets in Parquet and CSV with SHA-256 checksums, for anyone to reuse. About the datasets
Privacy
No ads and no tracking.
Messy feeds
TTC trip IDs change between polls, so trains are tracked by vehicle label. Bus trip IDs do not join the schedule, so each bus is matched to a route by position and heading: 96% of the fleet in about 0.3 seconds. More in the README
Built with
Python 3.12, PostgreSQL 16, dbt, Airflow, FastAPI, MapLibre GL JS, Docker and JavaScript.

Reliability

Riders mostly want to know one thing: how long will I really wait? Torontrack measures the gap between trains at every platform and compares it with the schedule.

The same trains run at every position of the slider. Only their spacing changes. Extra wait is how much longer a rider who turns up at a random moment waits than the schedule promises, and it is what the monthly report card grades, from A (up to 30 seconds) to F (beyond 4 minutes).

Measured since
9 October 2026, from train and vehicle observations. These are estimates from predicted arrival times, not official TTC statistics.
Service seen
A median of 98% of scheduled bus and streetcar service, across 3,021 route-direction days. On the first evening of trip-level checks, 98.6% of 13,580 scheduled trips were seen.
Unreported delays
When the TTC reports nothing but neighbouring platforms show long waits, the line is marked as a possible delay, not confirmed by the TTC.
Findings page
The same data answers wider questions: rain and Bike Share trips, snow and subway delays, the main causes of subway delay, potholes after freeze and thaw, beach water after rain, and inspection results by a restaurant's age. See the results on the live site
